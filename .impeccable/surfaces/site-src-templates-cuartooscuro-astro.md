# Surface brief: plantilla «Cuarto oscuro»

Scope: second landing-page template for landing-forge, rendered from `site/src/data/business.json` alone; selected with `--template cuarto-oscuro`. Visitor mode: Persuade. Demo shown to the business owner (buyer) first on a phone; end visitors are the business's customers.

Action: reserve/contact (Instagram, phone) and get directions. Proof: the business's own photos, literal review quotes, rating, hours.

Constraints: works for any business (1–8 photos of mixed aspect, 0–5 reviews, hours may be missing). No invented claims. Original template stays as `clasica`.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a darkroom session that develops the business's own photographs: every print starts as a blank enamel-white sheet and emerges to full colour. It refuses the restaurant-landing default of a photo hero, a headline and a row of cards.

OWN-WORLD: Safelight amber ground (#F2A60C family) owning the whole surface as flat matte fiber paper; dense black #0E0E0E and deeproom brown #4B2E07 ink; enamel white #F6F1E6 only for trays and print borders; silver grays only inside undeveloped prints. Grease-pencil capitals (Permanent Marker) for headlines, Chivo Mono for body and labels, DSEG7 LCD digits for times and ratings, Big Shoulders condensed caps for bottle-style labels. Hairline black 1.5px rules, square corners, outlined secondary buttons, filled amber-bright primary with black border.

STORY: The owner sees their place appear out of the paper, reads what customers actually say as test-strip evidence, and taps the tear-free primary action to reserve.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top safelight strip (name, station links 01–06, Reservar). Left 55%: eyebrow label, grease-pencil headline at 3 lines max, mono subheadline, primary "Reservar" + outlined "Cómo llegar". Right 45%: an enamel tray holding the lead photo that develops on load, an LCD timer above it reading today's hours, rating as LCD digits. Bottom edge: the 01–06 station strip beginning, cut by the fold.

FORM: Challenger adopted by the user: signals-instruments-darkroom-safelight-bay (catalog), fused with the product's photos and reviews. seed:969e441d33624e0b. Signature interaction: each print develops (blank → ghost → full colour) as it enters the viewport while its station timer counts; reduced motion shows prints developed. Motion grammar: one continuous emergence, 2.4s ease-out, nothing else animates.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Translations named
- Safelight hue stays the darkroom's amber for every business; the business's identity arrives through its photographs developing to full colour, not through recolouring the ground.
- Stations: 01 Exponer (hero), 02 Tira de prueba (value prop), 03 Revelar (services), 04 Fijar (reviews), 05 Lavar (story + FAQ), 06 Secar (gallery on drying line + visit + close).
